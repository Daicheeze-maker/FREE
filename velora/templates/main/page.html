{% elif slug == 'privacy' %}

We collect only what's needed to fulfil your orders: your name, email, delivery addresses and order history. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.

This is a demonstration store: payments are simulated and we never collect card numbers, CVVs or bank credentials. Newsletter subscribers can ask to be removed at any time.

{% elif slug == 'faq' %}

Is payment real?

No — every order is paid with demo credit (each buyer starts with ₱500,000). Nothing is charged.

Why are some prices marked “≈”?

If you pick a display currency other than PHP we convert using daily reference rates from the European Central Bank via the Frankfurter API. You're always charged in PHP.

Where do the photos come from?

Photos come from Unsplash, Pexels and Openverse, free libraries of photography. Photographers are credited on the About page and under product photos where required.

How does engraving work?

On watches that offer it, tick Add a personal engraving and type up to 20 characters. It is engraved on the case back for the fee shown. Engraved watches can't be returned unless faulty.

Can I choose the size and colour?

Yes. Pick a case size and a colour on each watch's page; the choices you made show in your bag and on your order.
