{% if slug == 'about' %}

Velora Market is a small Manila team with a big wardrobe. We curate clothing, watches, fine jewelry and a hand-picked selection of cars — and instead of hiring an agency, we model every piece ourselves. What you see on the site is how it really looks on real people.

What we stand for

  • Honest photos, worn by our own team.
  • Authentic watches and certified jewelry.
  • Real reviews from verified buyers only.
{% elif slug == 'shipping' %}

We deliver across the Philippines. Metro Manila orders placed before 2pm usually arrive within 48 hours; provincial deliveries take 3–7 business days. Watches and jewelry ship insured.

Rates

A flat fee of {{ fmt_base(15000) }} applies, and delivery is free on orders over {{ fmt_base(free_ship_minor) }} after discounts. (These values are configurable by store administrators.)

Cars

Cars are handed over at our Taguig showroom or delivered by our team in Metro Manila after paperwork is complete.

Tracking

Every order has a live tracker under My account → Orders.

{% elif slug == 'returns' %}

Clothing, watches and accessories can be returned unworn with tags within 30 days for a full refund. Earrings can't be returned for hygiene reasons unless faulty.

Car reservations can be cancelled before handover. Any order can be cancelled from your account while it's still Pending or Confirmed.

{% elif slug == 'privacy' %}

We collect only what's needed to fulfil your orders: your name, email, delivery addresses and order history. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text.

This is a demonstration store: payments are simulated and we never collect card numbers, CVVs or bank credentials. Newsletter subscribers can ask to be removed at any time.

{% elif slug == 'terms' %}

Velora Market is a demonstration e-commerce application. No real goods are sold and no money changes hands. Prices shown in currencies other than PHP are estimates based on published reference rates and are not the amount charged.

{% elif slug == 'faq' %}

Is payment real?

No — checkout uses a clearly labelled demo payment that simulates approval or decline. Nothing is charged.

Why are some prices marked “≈”?

If you pick a display currency other than PHP we convert using daily reference rates from the European Central Bank via the Frankfurter API. You're always charged in PHP.

Who are the models?

Our own team! Every product photo is styled and modelled in-house.

Where does nutrition data come from?

Gourmet gift items can be enriched with data from Open Food Facts, a free, collaborative food products database.
